# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in the MAIT project to be respectful and without harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate, also when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the people using MAIT.
- Take responsibility for your mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission.
- Trolling, or deliberately disrupting discussions.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in this repository and in every MAIT project space, and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **support@mait.sh**. All reports are reviewed and handled confidentially. The MAIT Team may remove, edit or reject comments, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may block people from the project temporarily or permanently.
