# Security policy

## Report a vulnerability privately

Email **security@mait.sh**.

Do not open a public issue, discussion or pull request for:

- a security vulnerability;
- leaked or exposed credentials, tokens or keys;
- personal data, yours or anyone else's.

If you posted something like this in a public issue by mistake, email security@mait.sh and we will help remove it.

## What to include

- The affected component (for example the `aidcx` launcher, the MAIT image, an MCP server or the portal) and its version (`aidcx version`).
- Your platform: Linux, macOS on Apple Silicon or Windows with WSL 2.
- What the issue is and its impact: what an attacker can do, and what they need first.
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept.
- Whether you know of it being exploited or disclosed elsewhere.
- How we can reach you, and whether you want to be credited.

Do not send real credentials or other people's data. Redact them, or describe them.

## What happens next

We acknowledge reports on a best-effort basis and handle them confidentially. MAIT is in beta, and there is no promised response or fix time. Please give us a reasonable time to fix an issue before you disclose it publicly.
